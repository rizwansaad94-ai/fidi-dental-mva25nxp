How a call goes

  1. Greets the caller, says it is an AI assistant, and asks permission before recording.
  2. Detects English or Spanish and continues in that language.
  3. Urgent, painful or distressing calls go straight to your team, with a reminder to call 911 for life-threatening emergencies.
  4. Answers hours and directions using only the published information.
  5. Captures name, callback number, preferred day, language and new or current patient. Reads it back. Never promises a time slot.
  6. Clinical, cost and coverage questions: “That’s a question for our team,” plus a callback request.

It never diagnoses, gives medical advice or quotes fees. Demo uses fake test data only. A production version would use only vendors that sign a Business Associate Agreement (BAA).
